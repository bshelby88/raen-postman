Clicking a paid request costs nothing

Every paid endpoint returns HTTP 402 Payment Required with a base64 payment-required header containing a signed challenge. Hit the request in Postman → decode the header → you see exactly who gets paid (payTo), how much, and through which facilitator. Settle with any x402 client (USDC on Base, eip155:8453) and resend with X-PAYMENT to get the real result.
